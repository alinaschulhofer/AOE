Executive
Coaching

1:1 Engagement

Private, one-on-one coaching for senior executives, founders, and high-achieving professionals who are ready to close the gap between where they are and the level of performance, fulfillment, and leadership they know is available to them.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, this engagement goes beyond strategy and skill-building. It addresses the internal structures — the psychological patterns, relational dynamics, and unconscious drivers — that either accelerate or quietly limit what you are able to achieve and sustain.

What to Expect

Each engagement is entirely bespoke. We begin with a deep diagnostic conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles — visible and invisible — that are shaping your experience. From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs.

Deep psychological and performance assessment
Identification of unconscious patterns limiting growth
Emotional regulation and nervous system resilience
Leadership presence and relational intelligence
Vision clarification and authentic goal architecture
Ongoing accountability and refinement

03

Signature
Educational
Series

Speaking · Workshops

Three proprietary educational programs, each addressing a distinct layer of the high-performance ecosystem. Delivered by Dr. Schulhofer personally — through speaking engagements or workshops, virtually or in-person — these series combine applied teaching, facilitated dialogue, and reflective engagement to create structured spaces for lasting change.

Each series is typically structured as four live sessions of 60–90 minutes, with intensive one- or two-day workshop formats also available.

Inner Architecture of High Performance™

Focuses on the internal foundations of high performance — supporting individuals in developing core psychological capacities including emotional intelligence, self-regulation, accountability, and resilience. Addresses authenticity, identity, nervous system regulation, and the translation of insight into sustained action.

Architecture of Leadership™

Examines the psychological demands of leadership and responsibility — supporting leaders in understanding how personal history shapes leadership behavior, navigating authority and relational complexity, and cultivating the capacity to inspire performance, coherence, and trust across diverse teams.

High-Performance Culture Series™

Explores the relational and systemic dynamics that shape team and organizational culture over time. Addresses how leadership, values, communication, and lived experience shape culture — and how individuals at every level can act as conscious instruments of transformation.

Speaking engagements and conference keynotes
Executive workshops — half-day, full-day, or two-day intensives
Multi-session programs (4 × 60–90 min) for organizations
Virtual or in-person delivery
All series delivered by Dr. Schulhofer

Concierge
Therapy

Private · Discreet

Private clinical psychotherapy for individuals who require the highest level of discretion, flexibility, and personalized care. Designed for those whose lives and schedules demand a therapeutic relationship built entirely around their needs — and whose inner work deserves the same level of excellence they bring to everything else.

Concierge therapy with Dr. Schulhofer integrates deep psychological work with performance science, offering a rare combination of clinical rigor and lived understanding of what it means to operate at the highest levels.

The Experience

Engagements are completely tailored — in format, frequency, modality, and location. In-person, remote, and intensive formats are available. All enquiries are handled with absolute confidentiality.

Deeply personalized clinical psychotherapy
Trauma-informed, psychodynamic, and evidence-based approaches
Nervous system regulation and somatic integration
Couples and relational work
Intensive formats for accelerated transformation
Absolute confidentiality and discretion
