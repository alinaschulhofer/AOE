Executive
Coaching

1:1 Engagement

Private, one-on-one coaching for senior executives, founders, and high-achieving professionals ready to close the gap between where they are and the level of performance, fulfillment, and leadership they know they're capable of.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, this engagement goes far beyond strategy and skill-building. It addresses the internal structures — the psychological patterns, relational dynamics, and unconscious forces — that either accelerate or limit what you are able to achieve and sustain.

What to Expect

Each engagement is highly individualized. We begin with a deep, 90-minute conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles — visible and invisible — that are shaping your experience. From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs. All engagements are retainer-based and structured over a minimum of three months, allowing for the depth, continuity, and trust that meaningful transformation requires.

Identification of unconscious patterns limiting growth
Relational dynamics and emotional intelligence
Leadership presence, clarity, and decision-making
Navigating pressure, burnout, and complex organizational demands
Clarification of vision, values, and legacy
Ongoing strategic support and personal accountability

Investment details are available upon inquiry.

Concierge
Therapy

Private · Discreet

As a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York, I offer a concierge-style approach to psychotherapy for those seeking highly personalized, flexible, and discreet psychological care — available virtually for individuals, couples, and families across both states.

Grounded in a relational psychoanalytic approach, this work goes beyond symptom management to support deep, lasting transformation and genuine wellbeing. I intentionally maintain a limited caseload — offering clients dedicated attention, flexible scheduling, and seamless coordination with other providers when needed.

This model is best suited for those who value depth and a therapeutic relationship that can adapt to the realities of a high-demand life.
