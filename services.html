Executive
Coaching

1:1 Engagement

Private, one-on-one coaching for senior executives, founders, and high-achieving professionals ready to close the gap between where they are and the level of performance, fulfillment, and leadership they know they're capable of.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, this engagement goes far beyond strategy and skill-building. It addresses the internal structures — the psychological patterns, relational dynamics, and unconscious forces — that either accelerate or limit what you are able to achieve and sustain.

What to Expect

Each engagement is highly individualized. We begin with a deep, 90-minute conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles — visible and invisible — that are shaping your experience. From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs. All engagements are retainer-based and structured over a minimum of three months, allowing for the depth, continuity, and trust that meaningful transformation requires.

Relational intelligence and emotional dynamics

Concierge
Therapy

Private · Discreet

As a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York, I offer a concierge-style approach to psychotherapy for those seeking highly personalized, flexible, and discreet psychological care — available virtually for individuals, couples, and families across both states.

Grounded in a relational psychoanalytic approach, this work goes beyond symptom management to support deep, lasting transformation and genuine wellbeing. I intentionally maintain a limited caseload — offering clients dedicated attention, flexible scheduling, and seamless coordination with other providers when needed.

This model is best suited for those who value depth and a therapeutic relationship that can adapt to the realities of a high-demand life.

The Experience

Relational psychoanalytic, trauma-informed, and evidence-based approaches

04

Education

Speaking · Workshops

Keynotes, workshops, and educational series grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework — each translating psychological insight into practical application, and equipping individuals and organizations to perform, lead, and relate more effectively.

In addition to three proprietary signature series, customized keynotes and bespoke educational engagements are available for organizations, conferences, and leadership teams. I deliver every engagement personally, virtually or in-person. Format, structure, and scope are tailored to the audience and context — details available upon inquiry.

Inner Architecture of High Performance™

This series focuses on creating the internal conditions that support sustained high performance and well-being across domains and roles. Its aim is to help develop psychological and relational capacities as foundations, emphasizing preventative inner change, alignment, and long-term sustainability.

Architecture of Leadership™

Architecture of Leadership focuses on developing the inner and relational architecture required to lead more effectively and consciously. This series supports leaders in seeing themselves within the system, understanding how personal history shapes leadership behavior, and cultivating the capacity to inspire performance, coherence, and trust across diverse teams.

High-Performance Culture Series™

The High-Performance Culture Series focuses on the organizational and relational conditions that sustain excellence over time. The series examines how leadership, values, communication, and lived experience shape culture, and how individuals at all levels can act as instruments of transformation — consciously or unconsciously influencing the system they are part of.
