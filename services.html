01

Executive
Coaching

1:1 Engagement

Private, one-on-one coaching for senior executives, founders, and other high-achieving professionals who are ready to close the gap between where they are and the level of performance, fulfillment, and leadership they know they're capable of.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, this engagement goes beyond strategy and skill-building. It addresses the internal structures — the psychological patterns, relational dynamics, and unconscious forces — that either accelerate or limit what you are able to achieve and sustain.

What to Expect

Each engagement is highly individualized. We begin with a deep 90-minute conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles — visible and invisible — that are shaping your experience. From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs. All engagements are retainer-based, initially structured over a minimum of three months, allowing for depth, continuity, and meaningful transformation.

This work is tailored to the individual and may include, but is not limited to:

Identification of unconscious patterns limiting growth
Relational and emotional dynamics
Leadership presence and emotional intelligence
Strengthening leadership effectiveness, clarity, and decision-making
Support for leaders navigating pressure, burnout, and complex organizational demands
Clarification of vision and legacy
Ongoing strategic, personal support, and accountability

Details and investment are shared upon inquiry.

Signature
Educational
Series

Speaking · Workshops

Engaging courses, seminars, keynotes, and workshops grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework. Each offering translates psychological insight into practical application, equipping individuals and organizations to perform, lead, and relate more effectively.

Three proprietary educational series, each addressing a distinct layer of the high-performance ecosystem. Delivered by Dr. Schulhofer personally — through speaking engagements or workshops, virtually or in-person — these series combine applied teaching, facilitated dialogue, and reflective engagement to create structured spaces for lasting change. Each is typically structured as four live sessions of 60–90 minutes, with intensive one- or two-day workshop formats also available.

Inner Architecture of High Performance™

Focuses on the internal foundations of high performance — supporting individuals in developing core psychological capacities including emotional intelligence, self-regulation, accountability, and resilience. Addresses authenticity, identity, nervous system regulation, and the translation of insight into sustained action.

Architecture of Leadership™

Examines the psychological demands of leadership and responsibility — supporting leaders in understanding how personal history shapes leadership behavior, navigating authority and relational complexity, and cultivating the capacity to inspire performance, coherence, and trust across diverse teams.

High-Performance Culture Series™

Explores the relational and systemic dynamics that shape team and organizational culture over time. Addresses how leadership, values, communication, and lived experience shape culture — and how individuals at every level can act as conscious instruments of transformation.

04

Concierge
Therapy

Private · Discreet

As a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York, Dr. Schulhofer offers a concierge-style approach to psychotherapy for those seeking highly personalized, flexible, and discreet psychological care. She is available for virtual therapy sessions for individuals, couples, and families across both states.

Grounded in a relational psychoanalytic approach, work with Dr. Schulhofer goes beyond symptom management to support long-lasting transformation and overall wellbeing. To provide the highest level of care, she intentionally maintains a limited caseload — offering clients dedicated time, flexible scheduling, and seamless coordination with other providers when needed.

This model is best suited for individuals who value depth and a therapeutic relationship that can adapt to the realities of a high-demand life.

The Experience

Virtual sessions only
Individual, couples, and family therapy
Trauma-informed, psychodynamic, and evidence-based approaches
Initial session: 90 minutes · Subsequent sessions: 50 minutes
Frequency determined collaboratively based on individual needs
Intensive or extended sessions available for accelerated progress
Direct contact information provided to all clients
Retainer-based concierge arrangement or fee-for-service
