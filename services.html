Executive
Coaching

1:1 · High Performance

Private, one-on-one coaching for those operating at the highest levels of their field — executives, founders, athletes, creatives, and other ambitious professionals committed to closing the gap between where they are and what they're truly capable of.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, this engagement goes far beyond strategy and skill-building. It addresses the internal structures — the psychological patterns, relational dynamics, and unconscious forces — that either accelerate or limit what you are able to achieve and sustain.

What to Expect

Each engagement is highly individualized. We begin with a deep, 90-minute conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles — visible and invisible — that are shaping your experience. From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs. All engagements are retainer-based and structured over a minimum of three months, allowing for the depth, continuity, and trust that meaningful transformation requires.

Identification of unconscious patterns limiting growth
Relational dynamics and emotional intelligence
Leadership presence, clarity, and decision-making
Navigating pressure, burnout, and complex organizational demands
Clarification of vision, values, and legacy
Ongoing strategic support and personal accountability

Investment details are available upon inquiry.

Concierge
Therapy

Virtual · Private

As a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York, I offer a concierge-style approach to psychotherapy for those seeking highly personalized, flexible, and discreet psychological care — available virtually for individuals, couples, and families across both states.

Grounded in a relational psychoanalytic approach, this work goes beyond symptom management to support deep, lasting transformation and genuine wellbeing. I intentionally maintain a limited caseload — offering clients dedicated attention, flexible scheduling, and seamless coordination with other providers when needed.

This model is best suited for those who value depth and a therapeutic relationship that can adapt to the realities of a high-demand life.

The Experience

Virtual sessions — individuals, couples, and families
Relational psychoanalytic, trauma-informed, and evidence-based approaches
Extended initial consultation · ongoing sessions tailored to your needs
Intensive and extended sessions available
Direct contact with me at all times
Retainer-based concierge arrangement or fee-for-service

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Coaching is a non-clinical process focused on goals and personal development, designed to help high-functioning individuals achieve personal and professional ambitions. Therapy focuses on addressing issues related to psychological diagnoses or mental health conditions. While my background as a clinical psychologist informs my work, coaching is not intended as a substitute for therapy. If at any point during coaching clinical support is needed, I will provide appropriate referrals.
While many coaches offer valuable insight and support, my approach is grounded in over a decade of formal academic and clinical training. As a licensed psychologist, my work is informed by rigorous doctoral and postdoctoral education, which required thousands of hours of supervised experience, clinical assessment, and ongoing professional development. This foundation allows me to go far beyond surface-level mindset work — I am trained to identify and work with complex psychological patterns, such as unconscious defense mechanisms, attachment dynamics, and emotion regulation difficulties, that often lie beneath performance blocks. My background enables me to hold space for deep transformation while integrating high-performance strategies in a way that is both effective and evidence-based.
I work mainly with high-performing individuals who are navigating personal or professional transitions, burnout, leadership challenges, or simply have a desire for deeper fulfillment and want to work toward actualizing their maximum potential. This includes executives, entrepreneurs, creatives, founders, athletes, and leaders operating in high-stakes environments — anyone who is ready to move beyond performance and into alignment.
Coaching engagements are structured over a minimum of three months to account for the depth, continuity, and trust that meaningful transformation requires. Most clients choose to continue beyond the initial period as the work deepens. Consulting engagements are customized based on organizational needs. Therapy is structured according to individual goals and treatment progress.
Coaching and consulting are retainer-based, reflecting the depth and continuity of the engagement. Investment details are discussed during an initial consultation. Fee-for-service arrangements are available for therapy clients seeking clinical services.
Coaching and consulting are available worldwide. I offer concierge psychotherapy virtually to clients in Florida and New York. International clients seeking clinical therapy support will be referred to qualified resources in their location.
Readiness is less about circumstances than about willingness — the willingness to be honest with yourself and engage with depth. If you sense that external achievement is no longer enough, or that something internal is limiting what you are able to create or sustain, this work may be exactly what you are looking for. The initial consultation is the right place to begin that conversation.
Notice Coaching and consulting are non-clinical services focused on growth, leadership, and performance. They do not constitute psychotherapy, mental health treatment, or crisis services. Where therapeutic support appears more appropriate, referrals to qualified professionals can be provided.
