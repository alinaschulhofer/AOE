Psychotherapy Intensives

Psychotherapy intensives offer a focused, in-depth therapeutic experience beyond the structure of traditional weekly sessions. Designed for individuals and couples seeking greater depth, continuity, or momentum in their therapeutic work, intensives provide extended time for meaningful psychological exploration in a contained and supportive setting.

An intensive may be particularly beneficial for those navigating
  • Significant life transitions
  • Interpersonal or relational crises
  • Trauma or experiences that are difficult to explore in shorter sessions
  • Recurring psychological or relational patterns
  • A desire for deeper personal or relational growth
  • Professional or personal schedules that make weekly psychotherapy challenging

Concierge Psychotherapy is a flexible, individualized approach to psychological care for those seeking greater accessibility, continuity, and support beyond traditional appointment-based treatment.

The arrangement includes up to six hours of clinical support per month, which may be allocated flexibly according to the client’s evolving needs and preferences.

Rather than following a predetermined session structure, clients may choose shorter, more frequent appointments or longer sessions, depending on what is clinically appropriate and mutually agreed upon.

For example, six hours may be arranged as six 60-minute sessions, four 90-minute sessions, or another combination of appointments and between-session support.

Concierge Benefits

Flexible Appointments

Session frequency and duration may vary from month to month, allowing the structure of care to adapt to evolving needs.

Extended Conversations

Additional time may be reserved around appointments, allowing sessions to continue beyond their scheduled duration when desired and clinically appropriate.

Priority Scheduling

Every reasonable effort is made to accommodate appointment requests within 24–48 hours, subject to availability. Immediate availability is not guaranteed.

Direct Between-Session Access

Clients may communicate by phone, email, or text for additional clinical guidance and support within agreed boundaries.

Month-to-Month Flexibility

No minimum commitment is required.

The six-hour allocation includes scheduled appointments and substantive between-session clinical support. Fees are paid in advance at the beginning of each monthly period. Unused hours do not roll over to subsequent months.

Emergency & Crisis Services

Concierge Psychotherapy includes direct between-session communication for non-emergency clinical guidance and support. However, this access does not constitute emergency or crisis-response services, and immediate responses cannot be guaranteed.

In the event of a mental health crisis or other emergency, please call 911, contact 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or proceed to the nearest emergency department.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

A 48-hour cancellation policy applies to all psychotherapy appointments, including extended sessions and intensives.

Appointments canceled or rescheduled with less than 48 hours’ notice are charged at the full scheduled fee.

For Concierge Psychotherapy, late-canceled or rescheduled appointments are deducted from the six-hour monthly allocation based on the originally scheduled appointment duration. Remaining hours may still be used during the same monthly period, subject to availability.
