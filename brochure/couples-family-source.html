Couples & Family Intensives

Couples and family intensives offer a focused, in-depth therapeutic experience beyond the structure of traditional weekly sessions. Designed for couples and families seeking greater depth, continuity, or momentum in their therapeutic work, intensives provide extended time for meaningful psychological exploration in a contained and supportive setting.

An intensive may be particularly beneficial for those navigating
  • Significant life transitions affecting the relationship or family
  • Relational or family crises
  • Trauma, ruptures of trust, or experiences that are difficult to explore in shorter sessions
  • Recurring conflicts or relational patterns
  • A desire for deeper connection and relational growth
  • Professional or personal schedules that make weekly psychotherapy challenging

Concierge Psychotherapy is a flexible, individualized approach to care for couples and families seeking greater accessibility, continuity, and support beyond traditional appointment-based treatment.

The arrangement includes up to six hours of clinical support per month, which may be allocated flexibly according to the couple’s or family’s evolving needs and preferences. The allocation includes scheduled appointments and substantive between-session clinical support.

Rather than following a predetermined session structure, clients may choose shorter, more frequent appointments or longer sessions, depending on what is clinically appropriate and mutually agreed upon.

For example, six hours may be arranged as six 60-minute sessions, four 90-minute sessions, or another combination of appointments and between-session support.

Concierge Benefits

Flexible Appointments

Session frequency and duration may vary from month to month, allowing the structure of care to adapt to evolving needs.

Extended Conversations

Additional time may be reserved around appointments, allowing sessions to continue beyond their scheduled duration when desired and clinically appropriate.

Priority Scheduling

Every reasonable effort is made to accommodate appointment requests within 24–48 hours, subject to availability. Immediate availability is not guaranteed.

Direct Between-Session Access

Clients may communicate by phone, email, or text for additional clinical guidance and support within agreed boundaries.

Concierge Psychotherapy Terms

Month-to-Month Flexibility

Concierge Psychotherapy is offered on a month-to-month basis without a minimum commitment. The arrangement automatically renews each month unless either party provides written notice of nonrenewal at least seven days before the beginning of the next billing period.

Monthly fees are payable in advance, and unused clinical hours do not carry over to subsequent months.

The monthly billing period begins on the enrollment date rather than following the calendar month. For example, if care begins on November 12, the next billing period begins December 12, and written notice of nonrenewal must be provided by December 5 to avoid renewal.

Emergency & Crisis Services

Concierge Psychotherapy includes direct between-session communication for non-emergency clinical guidance and support. However, this access does not constitute emergency or crisis-response services, and immediate responses cannot be guaranteed.

In the event of a mental health crisis or other emergency, please call 911, contact 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or proceed to the nearest emergency department.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Standard Psychotherapy Services

A 48-hour cancellation policy applies to standard psychotherapy appointments, including extended sessions and intensives.

Appointments canceled with less than 48 hours’ notice, as well as missed appointments (no-shows), are charged at the full scheduled fee.

Appointments may be rescheduled within the same week (Monday through Saturday) without an additional charge, subject to availability. To qualify for complimentary rescheduling with less than 48 hours’ notice, the replacement appointment must take place within the same week as the originally scheduled appointment.

Concierge Psychotherapy

Concierge Psychotherapy offers enhanced scheduling flexibility.

Appointments may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours’ notice without any deduction from the monthly allocation, subject to availability, provided the replacement appointment takes place within the same monthly billing period.

Additionally, clients are permitted one complimentary rescheduling with less than 24 hours’ notice per monthly billing period, provided the replacement appointment is successfully completed within that same billing period.

Additional rescheduling requests made with less than 24 hours’ notice, late cancellations, and no-shows are deducted from the six-hour monthly allocation based on the originally scheduled appointment duration.

For successfully rescheduled appointments, only the duration of the completed appointment counts toward the monthly allocation. Any remaining concierge hours may be used during the same monthly billing period, subject to availability.

Complimentary rescheduling is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed.
