Dr. Alina Schulhofer

My own quest for excellence began early in life and has since taken me across several countries on different continents. As a former young Olympic hopeful devoted to my craft of competitive swimming, I understand from the inside what it means to pursue greatness — the discipline, the sacrifice, and the particular loneliness that comes with striving at the highest level.

That personal journey led me to pursue a doctorate in clinical psychology, which has since allowed me to work with many remarkable individuals across industries — professional athletes, entrepreneurs, C-suite executives, scholars, and other leaders. Many at the height of their careers; just as many describing a sense of feeling lost, inadequate, unfulfilled, not living up to their true potential.

Few ever dared speak of their fears and doubts — often out of fear of judgement, or because they didn't feel they had a right to feel what they felt. After all, by ordinary standards, they had it all.

Having been confronted with these experiences firsthand, my understanding and capacity to work with the issues unique to high performance go far beyond clinical or academic training. I integrate lived experience with my professional background in working with individuals, couples, families, teams, and organizations.

My proprietary framework, the Architecture of Excellence™, guides all of my work — representing the emotional, psychological, and relational foundations that, in my experience, make up a world class individual.

Teams and organizations are complex systems that reflect the interplay of individuals with vastly different personalities, strengths, skills, and backgrounds. I bring the same depth of inquiry to systemic work as I do to individual practice.
