About

Alina Schulhofer, PsyD

Licensed Psychologist · Executive Coach · Former Elite Athlete

I am a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York, an executive coach, and the founder of Architecture of Excellence™ — a framework for high-performance living built at the intersection of depth psychology, neuroscience, and embodied practice.

My clinical work is psychoanalytically informed, meaning I work with the full complexity of the human being — not just symptoms or surface behaviors, but the underlying structures that shape how you think, relate, perform, and experience your life.

Background

I completed my doctoral training in clinical psychology at the Chicago School of Professional Psychology and have since worked across clinical, organizational, and coaching contexts. My background spans psychodynamic psychotherapy, trauma treatment, executive coaching, sports psychology consultation, and applied neuroscience.

Before entering psychology, I was a competitive athlete — an experience that shaped my understanding of performance, pressure, and the inner architecture that either limits or unlocks a person’s potential. That lived understanding now informs everything I do clinically and professionally.

I work with high-performing individuals — executives, founders, physicians, athletes, and driven professionals — who are successful by conventional standards but sense that something deeper is available to them. My work is not about fixing what is broken; it is about building what is possible.

Approach

I do not offer a scripted protocol or a productivity system. I offer a rigorous, individualized process of inquiry — one that takes seriously both the conscious and unconscious dimensions of human experience.

My approach integrates psychoanalytic depth, cognitive and behavioral science, somatic and nervous system work, and evidence-based coaching methodology. The result is a process that is intellectually serious, practically oriented, and attentive to the whole person.

I believe transformation is not an event. It is an architecture — built deliberately, layer by layer, through honest engagement with what is actually happening inside you.
