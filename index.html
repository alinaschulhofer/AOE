High performance is a form of art. Our inner architecture serves as the foundation for how we lead, perform, connect, and create. — DR. ALINA SCHULHOFER | FOUNDER, ARCHITECTURE OF EXCELLENCE™
High performers often don't struggle on the level of skill or talent — they struggle within.

We often mistakenly believe in the ability to improve our inner experience by changing our external world — that happiness will come with the next title, the next milestone, the next achievement.

On my own path, and in working with world class individuals across industries, I've learned that these misconceptions profoundly limit not only our inner contentment, but also put a ceiling on what we're able to achieve.

If our achievements are to be sustainable — both externally successful and internally satisfying — it is our responsibility to tend to our internal world in the same way we refine our skills, strategy, and physical conditioning.
