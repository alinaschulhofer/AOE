Private coaching for those operating at the highest levels of their field.

Dr. Alina Schulhofer offers private, 1:1 coaching for executives, founders, leaders, professional athletes, and others committed to closing the gap between where they are and what they’re truly capable of.

Grounded in the proprietary Architecture of Excellence™ framework, each engagement takes a comprehensive approach to understanding and developing the individual behind the performance. The work integrates psychological insight, executive coaching, strategy, and skill-building to explore the patterns and dynamics that influence leadership, decision-making, relationships, well-being, and personal and professional development.

Rather than focusing exclusively on immediate professional challenges or external achievements, the process considers the broader psychological and relational dimensions of high performance. Informed by Dr. Schulhofer’s extensive academic and clinical training as a licensed psychologist, this approach brings depth to the understanding of human behavior, relationships, and performance while remaining distinctly nonclinical.

Each engagement is highly individualized, combining structured exploration with targeted development and practical application. The objective is to strengthen the internal capacities and external strategies that support meaningful growth, enhanced functioning, and sustainable excellence.

The Architecture of Excellence™ Framework

The engagement is grounded in the proprietary Architecture of Excellence™ framework, which provides a structured foundation for understanding and developing the psychological, relational, and behavioral dimensions of high performance.

The process encompasses six interconnected pillars that inform the initial exploration, individualized strategy, and ongoing developmental work. The specific pillars and their applications are introduced and explored once the engagement has commenced.

While the framework provides a consistent methodological foundation, the areas of focus and direction of the work are tailored to each individual’s circumstances, objectives, and developmental needs.

The Process.

01

Exploration & Assessment

The initial four sessions are typically dedicated to an in-depth exploration of the individual’s personal and professional landscape, guided by the Architecture of Excellence™ framework. This structured exploratory process considers the individual’s current circumstances, professional responsibilities, personal history, strengths, challenges, relational dynamics, and aspirations.

Particular attention is given to identifying recurring patterns, internal and external obstacles, and opportunities for meaningful development. The purpose of this phase is to establish a comprehensive understanding of the individual and the factors shaping their leadership, performance, relationships, well-being, and personal and professional functioning.

This is a developmental exploration conducted within a nonclinical consulting relationship, not a psychological diagnostic assessment.

02

Individualized Strategy & Development

Following the initial exploratory phase, we collaboratively establish a targeted developmental strategy based on the individual’s priorities, objectives, and circumstances. Subsequent sessions integrate psychological insight, executive coaching, strategy, skill-building, and practical application.

Depending on the individual’s needs, the work may involve developing greater self-awareness, strengthening leadership capacities, improving relational effectiveness, navigating complex professional demands, and translating insight into meaningful behavioral change. The process remains flexible and responsive to evolving circumstances while maintaining continuity around the individual’s broader developmental objectives.

Written Developmental Strategy

Outlines the principal areas identified, the direction of our work, and the specific objectives we intend to address.

03

Integration & Continued Development

At the conclusion of the engagement, or another mutually agreed milestone, clients receive a comprehensive written integration report.

For clients who continue beyond the initial three-month period, the work evolves according to their developmental needs.

Comprehensive Integration Report

Synthesizes the principal areas explored, key insights, progress toward established objectives, and individualized recommendations — consolidating the work undertaken as a meaningful reference for continued personal and professional development.

Professional Scope

1:1 Private Consulting is a nonclinical service focused on personal growth, leadership development, and performance enhancement. Although informed by Dr. Schulhofer’s training and professional experience as a licensed psychologist, the engagement does not constitute psychotherapy, psychoanalysis, psychological diagnosis, mental health treatment, or crisis services. The consulting relationship does not establish a psychologist-patient relationship.
