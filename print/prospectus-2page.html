ARCHITECTURE OF EXCELLENCE™

Private coaching for those operating at the highest levels of their field — executives, founders, leaders, professional athletes, and others committed to closing the gap between where they are and what they’re truly capable of.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, each 1:1 engagement is an intensive process that integrates psychological insight, executive coaching, strategy, and skill-building to understand and develop the individual behind the performance. Professional Scope

1:1 Private Consulting is a nonclinical service focused on personal growth, leadership development, and performance enhancement. Although informed by Dr. Schulhofer’s training and professional experience as a licensed psychologist, the engagement does not constitute psychotherapy, psychoanalysis, psychological diagnosis, mental health treatment, or crisis services. The consulting relationship does not establish a psychologist-patient relationship.
