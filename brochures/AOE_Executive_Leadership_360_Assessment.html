Executive Leadership 360° Assessment.

ARCHITECTURE OF EXCELLENCE™

Effective leadership requires more than an understanding of one’s strengths, intentions, and capabilities. It requires an understanding of how those qualities translate into behavior, how leadership is experienced by others, and how individual patterns interact with the demands of an organization.

The AOE Executive Leadership 360° Assessment is a comprehensive, psychologically informed leadership evaluation designed to provide executives, founders, senior leaders, and other high-performing professionals with a deeper understanding of their leadership effectiveness and developmental opportunities.

Grounded in the Architecture of Excellence™ framework, the assessment integrates structured stakeholder interviews, competency-based analysis, and psychological insight to examine the individual, relational, and organizational dimensions of leadership.

The assessment emphasizes qualitative depth, contextual understanding, and actionable developmental insights.

The Assessment ProcessFive Stages
01

Leadership Exploration & Assessment Design

An initial in-depth consultation establishes the assessment objectives, organizational context, leadership responsibilities, and developmental priorities. A structured interview explores the leader’s self-perception, professional experiences, strengths, challenges, interpersonal dynamics, and aspirations.

02

Multi-Source Stakeholder Interviews

Approximately 8–12 professional stakeholders are identified through a collaborative selection process. Participants may include direct reports, peers, supervisors, board members, and other relevant individuals.

Confidential, structured interviews explore perceptions of leadership effectiveness, behavioral patterns, interpersonal impact, and developmental opportunities.

03

Competency-Based Analysis & Integration

Findings are systematically examined through the seven pillars of the Architecture of Excellence™ framework:

TRUTHCONNECTIONBEINGVISIONMEANINGCREATIONEXCELLENCE

The analysis identifies recurring themes, strengths, potential blind spots, differences between self-perception and stakeholder experiences, and relevant organizational influences.

04

Comprehensive Executive Assessment Report

A customized written report synthesizes the findings into a comprehensive leadership profile, identifying key strengths, developmental opportunities, behavioral and relational patterns, and strategic recommendations.

05

Executive Feedback & Development Planning

A private 90–120-minute feedback session provides an opportunity to review the findings, explore their implications, and establish actionable priorities for continued leadership development.

Two Pathways for Engagement
01

Standalone Executive Assessment

The assessment may be undertaken as an independent engagement, providing a comprehensive evaluation of leadership effectiveness and a written roadmap for continued professional development.

02

Assessment Followed by Private Consulting

The assessment may alternatively serve as the foundation for an individualized leadership development engagement through AOE’s 1:1 Private Consulting services.

Where continued consulting is elected, assessment findings inform a tailored developmental strategy focused on strengthening leadership competencies, addressing identified challenges, and translating insight into sustained behavioral and professional development.

Continued private consulting is optional and contracted separately.

Professional Scope & Confidentiality

The Executive Leadership 360° Assessment is a nonclinical leadership development and consulting service. Although informed by Dr. Schulhofer’s training and professional experience as a licensed psychologist, it does not constitute psychotherapy, psychological diagnosis, mental health treatment, or crisis services.

Stakeholder feedback is treated confidentially and synthesized to reduce individual identifiability. Complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed. The intended recipients and permitted uses of assessment findings are established before the engagement begins.

The assessment is intended for professional development and is not designed to serve as a standalone basis for high-stakes employment decisions.
